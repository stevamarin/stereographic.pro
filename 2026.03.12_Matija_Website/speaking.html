Speaking

I talk about building.

Specifically: what it looks like when a services business owner builds AI infrastructure for their own operation. Not theory — the actual systems, the actual numbers.

Talk topics

What I speak on

01

The Builder-CEO: AI Operations for Services Businesses

How a 10-person B2B agency built and deployed AI agents that handle intake, briefing, reporting, and production coordination. Practical, specific, numbers-backed. For agency owners, studio operators, and anyone running a services business with a small team.

45–60 min · Workshop or keynote
02

Building Without the Hype: A Practitioner’s Guide to AI in Operations

The gap between AI demos and AI in production is enormous. This talk covers what I’ve learned building and running agents in a real business — what works, what breaks, what the AI vendors won’t tell you.

30–45 min · Conference or podcast
03

Two Continents, Ten People, One System

On running a distributed creative operation across Serbia and the US — the logistics, the culture, the tools, and the AI layer that makes the time zones work. A case study in building distributed ops infrastructure without a large ops team.

30 min · Conference or panel

Full paragraph

Matt Simunec is the CEO of DME Studio, a B2B video agency with teams in Daytona Beach and Belgrade producing content for companies across North America and Europe. Over the past several years, he has built and deployed the AI agent infrastructure that runs DME’s internal operations — replacing manual processes in client intake, project briefing, production reporting, and content delivery with autonomous systems. Originally from Serbia, Matt now lives in Daytona Beach. He publishes The Build Log, a biweekly dispatch from the intersection of content production and AI, at mattsimunec.com.
